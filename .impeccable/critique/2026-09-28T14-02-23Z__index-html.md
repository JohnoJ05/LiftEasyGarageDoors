## Design Specificity Verdict
Genuinely authored for this business, not template-interchangeable. Mobile max-width:767px block is real content-editing. Density problem sits on top of otherwise disciplined work.

## What's Working
1. Persistent call bar - recognition-over-recall done right
2. Mobile content-pruning block - deliberate editing, documented with a code comment
3. Grid-divider "stamped fact" components - distinctive, on-brand

## Priority Issues

[P0] Fixed call bar can render the final CTA heading nearly invisible. .cta background and .mobile-call bar are both Site Yellow; the CTA heading's last line can sit at the bar boundary and become nearly illegible. Confirmed visually.

[P1] The 6-item "fast response" problem list is the densest unbroken block on the page. .problems only goes 2-column at min-width:480px; below that it's six full-width stacked rows.

[P1] Homepage repairs card (card A) is disproportionately dense vs its siblings (full itemized tick list vs one summary sentence each), duplicating repairs.html content.

[P1] Footer nav repeats all 6 header links verbatim on mobile with zero reduction - the one un-pruned duplicate on the page.

[P2] Photo placeholders are the largest non-informational blocks on the mobile page (known constraint, photos not ready).

[P3] Phone number/CTA appears 5+ times in one scroll, diluting rather than reinforcing by page end.

## Persona Red Flags
Casey (mobile): flat 6-row problem list, no grouping; photo placeholder reads like a broken image on fast scroll.
Jordan (first-timer): 4 of 5 trust-strip captions hidden on mobile, losing the "no call centre, no middleman" reassurance exactly where it's needed most.
