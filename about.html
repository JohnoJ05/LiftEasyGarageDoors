01 - THE BUSINESS

Over 30 years in the industry

I’m a time-served, qualified joiner who got into the garage door industry over 30 years ago. I trained as a door fitting engineer and have worked on many makes and types of garage doors and remote control systems over the past 30 years.

I’m passionate about my job, and I always aim to give a professional service and good customer service.

My business is now more a reactive one, as I now specialise in repairing and servicing, giving a fast response so that your door is mended and back in good working order. My aim is to keep your existing garage door working for as long as possible.

Steve Procter

The business is built around a straightforward, reliable service when you need it. When you call Lift Easy, you speak to Steve, and Steve is the one who turns up and fixes it.
