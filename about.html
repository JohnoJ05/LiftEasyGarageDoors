01 - The business

Over 30 years keeping garage doors working

Lift Easy specialises in reactive repairs, servicing and automatic garage door systems. With over 30 years' experience working with domestic garage doors, Steve has extensive experience with many popular makes and types of door.

The business is built around a straightforward, reliable service when you need it. When you call Lift Easy, you speak to Steve, and Steve is the one who turns up and fixes it.

A repair specialist

No new garage door installations, just repairs, servicing and keeping your existing garage door working safely and reliably, not pushing you towards a replacement.
