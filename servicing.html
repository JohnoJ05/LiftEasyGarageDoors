01 - What's included

Inspect, adjust and service

Steve can inspect, adjust and service your existing garage door and identify potential problems before they become bigger and more expensive.

Inspection of your existing door

A thorough look over the door and its moving parts (springs, cables, rollers, hinges, tracks, locks and fixings), checking for wear or damage.

Adjustments

Setting the door up so it opens and closes evenly, sits properly in the frame and isn't straining its springs or opener.

Full servicing

Cleaning and lubricating the moving parts and tightening fittings, so the door runs quietly and smoothly.

Spotting problems early

Worn parts are pointed out while they're still a small job, not after they've failed and left the door stuck.

Automatic openers

If your door is electric, the opener can be looked at too. See remotes & automatic doors.

Honest advice

If anything needs repairing, Steve will explain what's required and discuss the options with you.

02 - Warning signs

Is your door telling you something?

Doors rarely fail without warning. If you notice any of these, a service now could save a breakdown, and a bigger bill, later.

Grinding, squeaking or banging
Jerky or uneven movement
Heavier to lift than it used to be
Doesn't close fully or evenly
Frayed cables or worn parts
Opener struggling or slow
Noticed one of these? Give Steve a call. 07939 500544
03 - Why service

Small jobs now, fewer breakdowns later

Every time a garage door opens and closes, its moving parts wear a little. A regular service keeps your existing door safe, quiet and reliable, and spots that wear before it turns into a breakdown or a bigger, more expensive repair.

Lift Easy doesn't sell new doors, so the aim is always to keep the door you already have working well for as long as possible.
