03 - About Steve

Over 30 years keeping garage doors working

Steve has spent over 30 years repairing and servicing domestic garage doors and automatic garage door systems. When you call Lift Easy, you speak to Steve, and Steve is the one who turns up and fixes it.

A repair specialist

Lift Easy doesn't sell or install new garage doors. The focus is on repairing, servicing and keeping your existing door working safely and reliably, not pushing you towards a replacement.
